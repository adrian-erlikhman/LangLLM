# Venue hunt, domain C: student / pre-university venues

Compiled 20 Sept 2026 from official pages fetched that day (secondary sources are labelled as such). Window asked for: submission deadline between 25 Sept and 15 Dec 2026, decision within about 10 weeks or rolling with a stated turnaround. Authors: two Los Angeles high-school seniors plus a UCLA adjunct faculty co-author; completed LLM/authorship-attribution study with code and data.

Legend for the verdict lines: **ELIGIBLE** (all three authors can appear as written), **ELIGIBLE WITH CHANGES** (something about authorship or framing must change), **INELIGIBLE**, **OUT OF WINDOW**, **UNVERIFIED**.

Quotes are verbatim from the page or PDF named. "Not found" means the page(s) listed were fetched and do not state it.

## 1. Regeneron Science Talent Search 2027

- **URL:** https://www.societyforscience.org/regeneron-sts/ ; rules PDF https://sspcdn.blob.core.windows.net/files/Documents/SEP/STS/2027/Application/Official-Rules.pdf ; FAQ https://sciencetalentsearch.smapply.org/res/p/faq/
- **What is submitted:** Online application (essays, recommendations, transcript) plus a research report. Rules PDF, Appendix 2: "The paper should be 20 pages or less. There is no page minimum." "The title page, abstract and bibliography do not count toward the 20-page limit." "Appendices count toward the 20-page limit." "Research proposals, investigations not yet completed, literature reviews and essays are not eligible." "The Student Researcher is required to write the paper without the use of generative AI."
- **Deadline:** Rules PDF: "APPLICATIONS DUE ON THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 5, 2026 AT 8:00 PM EASTERN TIME!" Customer-support deadline "November 4, 2026 at 8:00 pm Eastern Time".
- **Decision:** "Top 300 Scholars Announced January 7, 2027"; "Top 40 Finalists Announced January 21, 2027"; finals week March 11–17, 2027.
- **Eligibility (quoted):** "Applicants must be in their final year of their secondary school ... must not have graduated from high school before the Regeneron STS application deadline, and must not have entered any previous STS." Citizenship: "A student (of any citizenship) who is enrolled in and attending secondary school AND who maintains full-time residence in the United States". **Team work:** "Applicants must complete individual research projects to enter Regeneron STS. Research conducted as part of a pre-collegiate student team project is not eligible for Regeneron STS. This includes any research or portion of research regardless of whether it has or will be submitted to any competition." "Students may not 'split' a team project and enter as individuals." "Students who have been part of a team project wishing to continue as an individual may only submit the work completed independently and may not submit any work completed with the teammates." **Adult co-authors:** "Research conducted alongside adult researchers in a research institution is permitted, but clarity and adequate knowledge of an individual's role and independence vs. work being done by the collective laboratory throughout the application is vital." **Journal publication / elsewhere:** "If an entrant is asked to waive copyright for the paper for publication in a peer-reviewed scientific journal, the entry is still considered eligible for Regeneron STS." arXiv: not addressed in the rules PDF.
- **Fees:** none stated on any page fetched.
- **Peer review type:** competition judging ("judging panel of doctoral scientists, mathematicians and engineers").
- **Publication?** No. "The Society does not publish entrant, scholar or finalist research papers in full."
- **Fit:** topic fine (computer science category exists); prestige highest on this list.
- **Risk / verdict:** **INELIGIBLE as a two-student paper.** The only route is for one student to enter only the portion done alone (rule 4.a.ii), and the pair must not then "combine individual research submitted to Regeneron STS and present it as a team in competition, publications or any forum" between November 2026 and June 2027, which would block co-authored journal submission of the same work. Not compatible with submitting the joint paper elsewhere this fall.

## 2. Journal of Emerging Investigators (JEI)

- **URL:** https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/guidelines ; https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/author-eligibility ; https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/faq ; https://emerginginvestigators.org/submissions/permissions-and-licensing
- **What is submitted:** manuscript on the JEI template ("hypothesis-driven research"). Page/word limit: not found on the guidelines page (template governs).
- **Deadline:** rolling; "Student authors may only have one manuscript at a time submitted to JEI."
- **Turnaround:** FAQ: "The length of the review process varies based on time of year, number of submissions we are currently processing in our system, and the completeness of revisions made by authors." Search-result snippet of the same FAQ adds "peak submission seasons (May through September and December to January) can result in delays of 2 weeks at each turnaround interval." No stated number of weeks; secondary sources (Harvard HMS Career Navigator listing) say 6–10 weeks to first decision, multiple revision rounds after.
- **Eligibility (quoted):** "All middle- and high-school students, aged 13 or older, are encouraged to submit"; "Initial submission of manuscript must occur prior to the student author's enrollment in university"; "All manuscripts at JEI must have at least two authors, one or more student authors and a senior author"; "A senior mentor must be included as the last author on all JEI manuscripts"; "A teacher, college/university professor, postdoctoral fellow, or senior graduate student may serve as a senior mentor if research was performed in a school or university." Multiple student authors: allowed ("one or more student authors"). No country restriction stated. **Prior/parallel submission:** "You may only submit the same manuscript to one journal at a time." "we are unable to accept work that has been or is intended to be submitted for publication at competitions, pre-prints, or other journals without clear and publicly stated copyright licenses/permissions"; "JEI can only accept previously published preprints if their copyright clearly allows for future publication or adaptation (CC-BY, for example)". Published under CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0.
- **Fees:** "JEI charges a $49 fee at the time of submission." "This $49 fee would be the only fee charged to submit and publish with JEI - there will NOT be a separate publication fee once the article is accepted." Waivers available.
- **Peer review:** PhD-scientist reviewers with written feedback (FAQ); blinding not stated.
- **Publication:** "All JEI articles are registered after publication and given a DOI." "JEI articles are not indexed." ISSN 2638-0870.
- **Fit:** good; the UCLA adjunct slots naturally as required last author. JEI's "hypothesis-driven" framing suits a stylometric attribution study if the research questions are stated as hypotheses. A CC-BY arXiv preprint is tolerated; a science-fair entry is discouraged.
- **Risk / verdict:** **ELIGIBLE.** Risks: turnaround is unstated and historically several months end to end; JEI's copyright clause conflicts with entering the same paper in a competition that claims rights; not indexed.

- **Publication:** DOI: not found. Indexing: "shown on research databases such as EBSCOhost" (https://nhsjs.com/about/the-nhsjs-advantage/).
