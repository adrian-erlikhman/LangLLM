# Venue hunt, domain A: NLP and ML conferences and their workshops

Researched 2026-09-20 from official pages only (conference/workshop sites, OpenReview, ACL portal, AAAI.org, ARR). Where a page did not state a fact it is written as "not found" or "not addressed" with the URL tried. Nothing below is guessed.

Window: submission deadline 25 Sept – 15 Dec 2026, notification within about 10 weeks.
Authors: two high-school first authors + an adjunct faculty co-author (UCLA).
Topic: LLM text analysis, stylometry, authorship attribution of LLM output, LLM evaluation and judging, multilingual NLP.

Legend for the eligibility verdict: **ALLOWED** (page permits it), **NOT ALLOWED** (page excludes it), **NOT ADDRESSED** (page silent; ask the chairs).

## 0. Headline findings

1. **No NeurIPS 2026 workshop is still open.** NeurIPS makes 29 Sept 2026 a mandatory, non-extendable author-notification date ("The mandatory author notification deadline is September 29, 2026", https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/WorkshopsGuidance), so every workshop closed between 27 Aug and 6 Sept. Twenty topical workshops were checked one by one (section 1); all closed. AI for Peace (Earshot's target) closed 21 Sept.
2. **No EMNLP 2026 workshop is still open** (conference is 24–29 Oct; the latest ARR-commitment date found was 4 Sept, MRL). Section 5.
3. **AAAI-27 workshops are the main in-window target**, with a common schedule of **20 Nov 2026 deadline / 2 Dec 2026 notification**, but the accepted list is not yet public (organizers notified 25 Sept; CFPs due to AAAI 2 Oct). Re-check https://aaai.org/conference/aaai/aaai-27/workshops-call/ after 2 Oct. Section 2.
4. **ARR October 2026 cycle (12 Oct, AoE) feeds NAACL 2027 and COLING 2027.** Meta-reviews 17 Dec (9.5 weeks); conference decisions 10 Feb 2027 (17 weeks). New rule: every submission needs a "qualified service contributor" (the UCLA co-author) or it goes into a lottery. All authors need OpenReview profiles with ORCID. Section 3.
5. **Student Research Workshops:** EACL 2027 SRW (deadline 15 Dec, notification 5 Jan) says "the first author MUST be a student (undergraduate or graduate)" — high school **not addressed**. NAACL 2027 SRW (deadline 11 Jan 2027, four weeks outside the window) is the only venue found that names high-school students explicitly: it welcomes "students at multiple stages, including high school, undergraduate, MSc/MA, and PhD students." Section 4.
6. **There is no LREC 2027.** ELRA's LREC page lists LREC 2026 (Palma) and an open call to host "LREC 2028 or LREC 2030"; LREC is biennial. The "17 Oct 2026" date on aggregator sites is LREC 2026's deadline (17 Oct 2025) shifted a year. Section 6.
7. **ICLR 2027 has no tiny-papers track**; its author guidelines mention "tiny papers" only to say they do not satisfy reciprocal reviewing. ICLR 2027 workshops take papers around 1 Feb 2027 (outside the window). The ICLR 2027 **main** deadline is 25 Sept 2026, on the window's first day, with decisions 16 Dec. Section 7.
8. Three AACL-IJCNLP 2026 workshops still have **ARR-commitment-only** tracks open (MultiPsyche 25 Sept, GeBNLP 29 Sept, PlurVA-LLM 15 Oct), useful only for a paper that already holds ARR reviews. Section 8.

- **Dual submission / arXiv (quoted):** "ARR will not consider any paper that is under review in a journal or another conference at the time of submission"; "There is no anonymity period or limitation on posting or discussing non-anonymous preprints while the work is under peer review" (unless the "no non-anonymous preprint" box is ticked).

## 10. Cross-cutting eligibility notes for a high-school first author
- No venue in this domain states an age or degree minimum for authors. The constraints that bite are (a) **student-track definitions** (AAAI SA and UC name undergraduate/graduate; EACL SRW says "undergraduate or graduate"; NAACL SRW names high school), (b) **reciprocal-reviewing / service-contributor rules** (ARR, ICLR, AISTATS, several NeurIPS workshops), which the UCLA adjunct can satisfy, and (c) **OpenReview profile requirements** (ARR desk-rejects without complete profiles incl. ORCID; ICLR requires profiles; non-institutional e-mails are moderated, so create both students' profiles weeks ahead).
- Registration student rates: no 2027 fee table has been published by AAAI, NAACL, COLING, EACL or ICLR as of 20 Sept 2026; whether a high-school student qualifies for a student rate is **not found** everywhere.
