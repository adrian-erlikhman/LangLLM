Every fact below is quoted from an official page in the six domain reports (`venues_A` to `venues_F` in this folder); "not addressed" means the venue states no rule on author status, which in practice means allowed.

## Ruled out, with the reason

- Every NeurIPS 2026 and EMNLP 2026 workshop: closed (NeurIPS notifications are mandated by 29 Sept).
- Regeneron STS: individual work only; a co-authored paper is ineligible and entering it would bar joint presentation until June 2027.
- NeurIPS High School Projects track: no 2025 or 2026 call; last ran 2024.
- AAAI-27 Student Abstract, ACM SAC SRC, CHI and SIGCSE student research competitions: undergraduate/graduate only.
- All ICDM 2026 workshops, ICMLA, DSAA, ASONAM 2026, CCS 2026 workshops, ACSAC, USENIX Security cycle 2 (26 Jan), NDSS main: closed or outside the window.
- JEI / NHSJS / IJHSR: months to decision, $49 to $350, not indexed; JEI refuses work that will be preprinted or sent elsewhere.
- LREC 2027 does not exist (biennial; next is 2028). ICLR 2027 has no tiny-papers track.
