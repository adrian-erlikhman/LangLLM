# Venue sweep G5 — remaining venues (checked 21 Sept 2026)

Scope: venues not covered in earlier reports (A–F, G1–G4). Paper: LLM authorship
attribution / multilingual stylometry / LLM-as-judge / machine translation as an
obfuscation attack. Authors: two high-school first authors plus an adjunct
faculty co-author (UCLA). Window: submission on or after 22 Sept 2026 and a
decision by about 15 Jan 2027, or rolling with a stated turnaround under ten
weeks.

Every fact below comes from an official page fetched today or, where marked
"(search snippet)", from a search-engine excerpt of an official page that could
not be fetched (Anubis / 403 / 404). "Not addressed" under eligibility means the
official pages contain no author-status rule at all, which in practice means a
high-school first author with a faculty co-author is allowed. Nothing checked
here excludes such an author explicitly.

Legend: **MEETS** = inside the window · **FAILS** = outside it · **TBA** =
not yet announced.

## 1. TACL — Transactions of the ACL  **MEETS (rolling; ~7–8 weeks nominal, delays flagged)**
- URL: https://transacl.org/index.php/tacl/about/submissions and /about
- Track: journal article (regular or survey).
- Deadline: monthly. "The next submission deadline for TACL is the first (1st)
  of the next month in the calendar, by 11:59pm Honolulu time." Next: 1 Oct
  2026, then 1 Nov, 1 Dec.
- Turnaround (stated): "Reviews will be due 3 weeks after a reviewing request
  has been made"; action editors have "7 days" to decide after reviews;
  "We unfortunately cannot guarantee that the timeline guidance given in the
  items above will be met for every submission." Homepage notice (2026):
  "Due to increased submission volume and limited reviewer availability, some
  papers may experience delays in the review process." Decision types: (a)
  accept, (b) minor revision, (c) major revision, resubmittable within 3
  months, (d) reject, no resubmission for 12 months. Papers rejected from
  ACL/AACL/EACL/NAACL/EMNLP are ineligible for 9 months from that deadline.
- Page limit and format: 10 pages maximum (surveys "must also keep to the same
  length limit as regular submissions"); official TACL style (rev. Dec 2021);
  LaTeX source required at publication.
- Review: double-blind for reviewers ("must not include author names,
  affiliations, or identifying acknowledgments"); action editors see authors.
- Eligibility: not addressed. No reviewer-obligation rule for authors appears
  on the submissions, about or homepage pages ("Reviewers will have at most 1
  paper to review at any one time" is a reviewer-side rule).
- Fees / in-person: "TACL imposes neither author processing charges or nor
  submission charges." Presentation slot at an ACL conference is optional.
- Proceedings / indexing: MIT Press, open access; ACL Anthology.
- Fit: strong, this is the field's own journal, and a 10-page cross-lingual
  attribution + MT-obfuscation study is squarely in scope.
- Credibility: top-tier; the highest-prestige option in this sweep. Risk is
  timing only, since the 2026 delay notice means the ~7-week nominal path is
  not guaranteed.

## 3. NEJLT — Northern European Journal of Language Technology  **MEETS (rolling; 90% first decision in 12 weeks; Letters faster)**
- URL: https://www.nejlt.org/ , /authorinfo/ , /review/
- Track: Full paper (six types: computationally-aided linguistic analysis,
  NLP engineering experiment, reproduction, resource, position, survey) or
  Letter.
- Deadline: rolling ("Log in and send your paper to NEJLT").
- Turnaround: review page reports 2022 first-decision "median: 69 days, mean:
  87 days, s.d.: 39.7"; the authorinfo/about pages (search snippet of the
  official site) state "the journal will provide a decision within eleven
  weeks for most manuscripts" and "a first decision on 90% of papers within
  twelve weeks", with a 2024 average of 58 days. Letters "are reviewed more
  quickly than full papers". "Accelerated review when providing recent prior
  reviews (<1yr) from top venues."
- Page limit / format: full papers "may be of any length, though we recommend
  about twelve pages (excluding references)"; Letters "up to six pages
  (excluding references)". LaTeX template at github.com/NEJLT/nejlt-template.
- Review: double-blind during review ("Author identity should be hidden");
  reviewer names may be revealed afterwards (signed reviews). Desk rejects for
  out-of-scope, low quality, plagiarism, or revealed author names.
- Eligibility: not addressed. "Journal extensions of prior work are not
  eligible for review. NEJLT submissions should be completely original work."
  "Welcomes manuscripts from anywhere in the world."
- Fees / in-person: "Free to publish, free to submit, free to read." No
  presentation.
- Proceedings / indexing: LiU E-Press, "automatically indexed and published in
  the ACL Anthology"; DOIs.
- Fit: strong; NLP engineering experiment or Letter type fits exactly.
- Credibility: solid, small, ACL-Anthology-indexed diamond OA journal; low
  volume (12 submissions in 2024), so less prestige than TACL but faster and
  more predictable.
