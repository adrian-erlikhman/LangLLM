# Venue scan G2 — other CS conferences, workshops and symposia with an October 2026 notification

Scope: peer-reviewed CS venues (not IEEE BigData 2026) whose author notification falls
1–31 October 2026 **and** whose submission deadline is on or after 21 September 2026.
Checked 20 September 2026 against official pages (WebFetch/curl) with web search only
to locate them. Where a page was JS-rendered or blocked (CSCI, ICCE, CCNC, InfPriv), the
raw bundle or a mirror was read and that is noted. Quotes are verbatim from the source.

Eligibility column: "not addressed" means the page states no author-status rule; none
of the venues below bars a high-school first author with a faculty co-author.

---

## A. October hits

### A1. CSCI 2026 — International Conference on Computational Science and Computational Intelligence
- URL: https://www.american-cse.org/csci2026/ (deadlines page is a React bundle; text read from
  `csci2026/scripts/app.*.js`); submissions via https://american-cse.org/drafts/
- When/where: "December16-18, 2026 - Las Vegas, USA", "Luxor - an MGM property"; "hybrid event".
- Deadline: "SUBMISSION OF PAPERS (for peer-review evaluation): October 6, 2026" (no time zone
  given; the page says "the paper submission deadline has been extended as outlined below").
- Notification: "NOTIFICATION OF ACCEPTANCE/REJECTION: REVOLVING DATE — Will be sent about 10 days
  AFTER the date of submission." So anything submitted 21 Sept–6 Oct is answered in October.
- Page limits (Springer one-column): Regular/"Late Breaking" "Between 12 and 15 pages"; Short
  "Between 6 and 11 pages"; Poster/Extended Abstract (= "Position Research Papers") camera-ready
  "should not exceed 2 IEEE style pages".
- Review: peer review, criteria "quality of research, quality of writing, and contribution to
  conference program and diversity"; only "Papers whose authors include one or more members of the
  program committee will be evaluated using the double-blinded review process" (otherwise
  single-blind). Rejected authors may send a rebuttal.
- Eligibility: not addressed. Allowed.
- Fees: on registration page (not captured); "at least one author for each accepted paper is
  required to register before the announced deadline".
- In person: hybrid, "combining a live, in-person experience with a virtual, online component".
- Proceedings: "All accepted papers of all Research Tracks will be published by Springer Nature",
  post-conference; claims Scopus, DBLP, EI Compendex, CPCI indexing.
- Fit: yes; CSCI-ISAI (AI) track covers NLP/LLM work.
- Reputation: **low-tier.** American Council on Science and Education / the WORLDCOMP lineage;
  ten-day rolling decisions, revolving deadlines, hybrid-by-default. Springer volumes are real
  and indexed, but a line on a résumé reads as a pay-to-publish venue to anyone who knows it.

### A3. WACV 2027 workshops (IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision)
- URL: https://wacv.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/Workshops (26 workshops) and
  https://wacv.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/CallForWorkshops
- Conference-wide rule: "Author notification (archival papers): October 30, 2026"; camera-ready
  "November 20, 2026, at 11:59 PM Pacific Time"; workshops "January 4th - 5th, 2027";
  "all presenters must attend in person" and be registered. Archival workshop papers go to the
  WACV 2027 Workshop Proceedings (IEEE Xplore, indexed separately from the main conference).
- Verified workshops with a deadline ≥ 21 Sept and October notification:
  - **SIGNAL — Source-Integrated Generative-content Attribution and Labeling**
    (https://signal.philab.fund/): "Oct 15, 2026 Submission deadline All tracks", "11:59 PM
    Anywhere on Earth"; "Oct 30, 2026 Author notification". Scope: "If your work touches
    watermarking, fingerprinting, provenance, or the benchmarks that measure them, in any carrier,
    it belongs here"; "We are inviting additional speakers on audio and speech, media forensics,
    and language models." Half-day, in person; archival camera-ready 20 Nov. Page limit and
    blind status not on the page (WACV template; assume 8 pages, double-blind as per WACV).
    Fit: **the closest fit in this whole scan** for LLM fingerprinting/attribution, though the
    audience is computer vision. Eligibility not addressed.
  - CV4EO (Earth observation, https://geoai.ornl.gov/cv4eo-wacv/): "02 October 2026 (Fri), AoE";
    notification 30 October 2026; "up to 8 pages"; double-blind. Fit: none.
  - WVAQ (image/video/audio quality, https://wacv2027-image-quality-workshop.github.io/):
    "October 12, 2026" → "October 23, 2026"; single-blind; in proceedings. Fit: none.
  - VisionDocs: 13 Oct → **2 Nov** (misses October). Data4VFM: 23 Nov → 5 Dec, non-archival,
    excludes text-only LLM work.
  - The other 21 workshops were not opened individually; the 30 Oct conference-wide notification
    applies to any of them that run an archival track, but none is text/NLP-facing by title.
- Fees: WACV registration (not captured). Reputation: WACV is a solid CVF/IEEE venue; workshop
  papers are a tier below main-track papers but are Xplore-indexed and respectable.

## C. Bottom line
Only one venue in this scan is both open after 21 Sept, decides in October, and is on topic:
**SIGNAL @ WACV 2027** (15 Oct AoE → 30 Oct, in-person Jan 4/5, Xplore). CSCI 2026 and
ICMLC 2027 also satisfy the dates but are low-tier. ARRSI and TrustAudio (AACL) fit the dates
but not the topic. Everything else either closed before 21 Sept or decides in November or later.
