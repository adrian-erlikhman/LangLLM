# Venue hunt, domain F: learning analytics, ed-tech, computational social science, web science, HCI, information science

Compiled 20 Sept 2026. Window: submission deadline 25 Sept – 15 Dec 2026, notification within ~10 weeks.
Authors: two high-school first authors + adjunct faculty co-author (UCLA).
Topics: which model wrote a student's essay (any language); LLM homogenization of writing; LLM-as-judge; misinformation provenance; multilingual NLP.

Method: web search plus fetches of the official pages named under each entry. Everything quoted is from the page cited. "Not found" means the page was fetched (or tried) and the item was not on it; the URL tried is given. The web-search budget for this session ran out partway through (200 searches), so a few items that could only be reached by search are marked accordingly.

Legend for the eligibility verdict:
- **Allowed** = the page has a rule and it admits a high-school first author with a faculty co-author.
- **Not allowed** = the page has a rule that excludes them.
- **Not addressed** = no rule on author status, affiliation, student status or age was found on the pages fetched. In practice, the venues below that are "not addressed" publish under ACM/AAAI/Springer authorship policies, which have no age or affiliation requirement; the practical constraints are the registration fee and the in-person presentation rule.
